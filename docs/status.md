# Status

Last updated 2026-11-12. Read this first when picking the project back up.

## Where things stand

- **Scope is decided:** round tube only, up to 3" OD, three axes (X, A, Z), boring catalog parts, about $1,130. See [design.md](design.md).
- **Chuck is in hand:** Vevor K11-250 10" three-jaw, 80 mm bore. Measurements below.
- **CAM spike works in software and on paper:** `cam/cope.py` makes G-code and a wrap template for round-on-round copes. The owner printed the template, cut by hand, and the fit was good. The G-code has not run on any machine.
- **Headstock is drawn, not cut:** two plates and a spacer, generated by `headstock/plates.py`. See [headstock.md](headstock.md).
- **Nothing is built.**

## Chuck as measured (2026-11-12)

The chuck arrived and matches the standard K11-250 back-mount pattern.

| Item | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Through bore | 80 mm (79.8 measured); 3" tube passes | Paperwork and calipers |
| Mounting | 3 x M12, tapped in the back face, 120 degrees apart | Measured (bolt major diameter 11.9 mm) |
| Bolt circle | 226 mm | Paperwork; tape gives 7 3/4" hole to hole, which works out to 227 mm |
| Hole clocking | 10 to 15 degrees off the jaws, not on a jaw or midway | By eye |
| Back register | Raised ring, 250 mm OD, 206 mm ID (so a 206 mm recess) | Paperwork; tape agrees |
| Grip range | Down to 1/4"; 1" tube is no problem | Paperwork and by hand |
| Runout | 0.08 mm on a test bar 50 mm out, from the paperwork row for chucks under 200 mm | Paperwork only, not measured |

| Recess depth | 5 mm on paper, 5.88 mm measured; keep the adapter boss under 5 mm | Paperwork and calipers |
| Body thickness | 80 mm (80.79 measured), without jaws | Paperwork and calipers |
| Weight | 49 lb (listing); box says 50 lb with wrench and packaging | Listing, not weighed |

## Next steps, in order

1. **Measure the slewing ring when it arrives** (011.10.150, ordered 2026-11-12) against the table under open risks: bore, bolt circles, hole size, race heights and offset, and where the teeth sit across the height. Update `headstock/plates.py` if anything differs.
2. **Order the A motor** (5:1 geared NEMA 23), then measure its shaft and mounting holes. Buy the pinion to suit and add the motor holes to the frame plate.
3. **Cut the plates** after 1 and 2: adapter plate by laser at SendCutSend, frame plate and spacer on the plasma table. The headstock design is in [headstock.md](headstock.md); DXFs come from `python headstock/plates.py`.
4. **CAM for arbitrary shapes from Fusion 360.** This does not depend on hardware and can start any time. Plan in [cam.md](cam.md): a small Fusion script exports the cut edges of a selected tube body as unwrapped loops, and the existing tool turns loops into G-code and a template. The owner confirmed tubes are modelled as hollow bodies. The Fusion script can only be tested by the owner running it inside Fusion.
5. Gantry, frame, torch quick mount (must match the one-bolt mount on the CrossFire Pro), second CPC cable, controller.

## Open risks

- **QtPlasmaC and tube cutting.** It is built for flat tables. Whether it accepts the inverse-time (G93) feed moves the CAM tool writes is untested. Fallback is plain LinuxCNC with simple torch control.
- **Slewing ring: 011.10.150 is the pick.** A supplier's 011-series table (an Amazon listing image the owner found 2026-11-12, not kept in the repo) gives: bore 100 mm, outer ring body 200 mm, gear tip diameter 214 mm, module 2, 105 teeth, tooth width 20 mm, total height 30 mm (each ring 25 mm, offset 5 mm), outer bolt circle 180 mm and inner bolt circle 120 mm with 8 x 9 mm holes each (M8), 5.5 kg. The 011.10.120 has an 80 mm bore, too small. Buy the version with plain through holes, not threaded (see next item). Price not confirmed. Measure it on arrival; the table's bolt-size column (M14) contradicts its 9 mm holes.
- **Weight.** Chuck plus ring is about 61 lb before plates and motor, so the machine should sit on its own wheeled cart.
- **Small thin tube speed.** 16 gauge on 1" tube may need the chuck to turn faster than an open-loop stepper likes.
- **Prices.** Most of the BOM is estimates. See [bom.md](bom.md) for which were checked.
